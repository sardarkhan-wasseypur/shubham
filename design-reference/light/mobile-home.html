Advocate Shubham Singh maintains an active litigation practice anchored across Delhi courts and specialized regulatory tribunals. The chamber prioritizes trial readiness, robust statutory construction, and procedural accuracy across multifaceted civil and criminal proceedings.

With an emphasis on structured drafting and plain-language advisory, representation is tailored to deliver dependable, timely, and objective counsel for individuals, public sector undertakings, and corporate bodies alike.

07 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which matters do you handle? +

The chamber handles trial defense, commercial and property litigation, matrimonial and family disputes, Section 138 NI Act complaints, and trademark applications or oppositions.

Which courts do you practise in? +

Litigation is actively conducted before the Supreme Court of India, the Delhi High Court, all District Courts across the National Capital Territory, and statutory tribunals including NCLT and NCDRC.

How can I contact the office? +

You can initiate an inquiry via the chamber contact form below or connect through phone and WhatsApp. Official consultation slots are confirmed subsequent to preliminary verification.

What should I prepare for a first consultation? +

Please bring copies of relevant notices, court orders, case filings, contracts, or statutory notices. A concise chronological timeline of key events is strongly recommended.

How do I enquire about a matter? +

Submit the preliminary consultation form below detailing the category of dispute. The chamber will review the matter regarding jurisdiction and availability.
