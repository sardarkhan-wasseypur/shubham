01

Introduction

Advocate Shubham Singh directs an independent litigation and advisory chamber headquartered in New Delhi. The chamber focuses extensively on trial court strategies, critical criminal defenses, civil remedies, and high-stakes appellate review before the High Court of Delhi and the Supreme Court of India.

Grounded in procedural precision, the practice advises high-net-worth individuals, institutional enterprises, and statutory instrumentalities across complex white-collar investigations, economic offences, corporate fraud inquiries, constitutional writs, and specialized arbitration references.

02

Professional Journey

Following rigorous formative years alongside senior jurists and designated senior counsel in the Capital, Adv. Singh established his independent chambers to cultivate an assertive, methodical litigation service tailored to nuanced courtroom demands.

The chamber regularly conducts evidentiary trials and urgent motion hearings throughout all major Delhi District Courts—including Tis Hazari, Patiala House, Saket, Karkardooma, Rohini, Dwarka, and the Special Rouse Avenue Court handling anti-corruption and financial investigations.

Experience & Core Practice

Synthesizing trial forensics with appellate agility, the chamber represents diverse client mandates ranging from pre-litigation risk exposure evaluations to constitutional writ remedies before high benches.

Practice Domain I

Criminal Defense & White Collar

Handling PMLA, CBI trials, SFIO summons, bail applications, quashing petitions under Sec 482 CrPC, and trial-level cross examinations.

Practice Domain II
