For case-specific queries, consult the advocate. What is a Succession Certificate? A Succession Certificate is granted by a civil court under the Indian Succession Act, 1925, certifying legal heirs entitled to realize debts and securities of a deceased person who died intestate. Is probate of a will mandatory in Delhi? Unlike statutory rules applicable in presidency towns, probate of a will executed in Delhi is generally not mandatory under Section 57 read with Section 213 of the Indian Succession Act, though it remains advisable for establishing clean title. How is child custody decided by Indian courts? The paramount consideration in any child custody proceeding under the Guardians and Wards Act, 1890 is the best interest and welfare of the child, overriding parental property-like claims. Can a daughter claim share in ancestral property? Yes. Following the 2005 amendment to the Hindu Succession Act, 1956 and the Supreme Court ruling in Vineeta Sharma v. Rakesh Sharma, daughters are coparceners by birth with equal rights in joint family property.
