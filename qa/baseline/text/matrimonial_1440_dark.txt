STAGE 0 1 Consultation & Settlement Assessment Confidential review of marital background, financial disclosures, and feasibility of mediated settlement. For case-specific queries, consult the advocate. What is the procedure for Mutual Consent Divorce? Under Section 13B of the Hindu Marriage Act, both parties jointly file a petition (First Motion) stating they have lived separately for at least one year. After a statutory period (or waiver), the Second Motion is recorded, leading to the dissolution decree. Can the six-month statutory cooling-off period be waived? Yes, the Supreme Court of India in Amardeep Singh v. Harveen Kaur held that the six-month period under Section 13B(2) is directory, not mandatory, and Family Courts can waive it where all settlement terms are concluded. How is interim maintenance calculated? Under the guidelines established in Rajnesh v. Neha by the Supreme Court, both parties must file comprehensive Affidavits of Assets and Liabilities. The court assesses status, income, financial needs, and commitments to fix a balanced amount. What is the difference between Judicial Separation and Divorce? Judicial separation releases spouses from the obligation to cohabit while keeping the legal status of marriage intact. Divorce permanently dissolves the marriage, allowing parties to remarry.
