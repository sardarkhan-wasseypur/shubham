For case-specific queries, consult the advocate. What is an interim injunction in civil law? An interim injunction (under Order XXXIX Rules 1 & 2 CPC) is a temporary judicial order preserving the status quo of a property or restraining a party from taking prejudicial action pending final decision of the suit. Is pre-institution mediation mandatory for commercial suits? Under Section 12A of the Commercial Courts Act, 2015, pre-institution mediation is mandatory for commercial suits unless the plaintiff seeks urgent interim relief. What is a Summary Suit under Order XXXVII CPC? A summary suit is an expedited procedure for debt recovery based on bills of exchange, hundis, promissory notes, or written contracts where the defendant must apply for leave to defend. How long does a civil decree remain valid for execution? Under the Limitation Act, 1963, an application for execution of a civil court decree must generally be instituted within 12 years from the date the decree becomes enforceable.
