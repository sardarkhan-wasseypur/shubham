For case-specific queries, consult the advocate. How long does trademark registration take in India? If an application encounters no objections or third-party oppositions, registration may be completed within 6 to 12 months. Contested proceedings may take longer depending on hearings. What is the difference between ™ and ® symbols? The ™ symbol denotes an unregistered mark for which protection is claimed or an application is pending. The ® symbol may legally only be used once the trademark registration certificate has been formally issued. What is the time limit to oppose a published trademark? Under Section 21 of the Trade Marks Act, a Notice of Opposition must be filed within four months from the date the application was advertised or re-advertised in the Trademark Journal. What is the difference between infringement and passing off? Infringement is a statutory remedy available exclusively for registered trademarks under the Trade Marks Act, 1999. Passing off is a common law tort protecting unregistered brand goodwill and reputation against deceptive misrepresentation.
