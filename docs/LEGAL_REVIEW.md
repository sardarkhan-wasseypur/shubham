## 1. Statutory Notice & Cheque Bounce (Section 138, Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881)
- **Factual Averments Published**:
  - The statutory legal demand notice must be dispatched within 30 days of receipt of the bank return memo.
  - The drawer must be provided a 15-day cure/payment period following receipt of notice.
  - If payment is not made within 15 days, a criminal complaint must be instituted before the competent Metropolitan Magistrate within 30 days of the cause of action arising.
  - Section 142(1)(b) allows condonation of delay upon sufficient cause shown.
  - Section 143A interim compensation up to 20% of the cheque value may be awarded during trial.
  - Vicarious liability under Section 141 applies to directors/partners in charge of business.
